Evidence Wall

The archive is the product.

Every public claim is classified before it is promoted. Verified is green, partially verified is amber, source pending is gray, private is blue, and do-not-publish is red/internal only.

Public logic

  • Verified achievements, public links, published initiatives, official project pages, and already public social evidence may be public.
  • Metrics without screenshots, partnership details without documents, and claims lacking source remain needs verification.

Protection logic

  • Family details, sensitive legal or financial details, intimate relationships, and unverified personal stories stay private.
  • Rumors, private phone numbers, private addresses, sensitive third-party data, and credibility-harming unsourced claims do not publish.
