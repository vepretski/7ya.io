THE OPERATING RULE

Evidence before amplification.

A statement does not become public truth because it sounds persuasive. It moves through a visible decision path.

01
“

Claim

State exactly what is being asserted. Remove hype, ambiguity, and narrative inflation.

02
⌁

Source

Attach the strongest available record: document, archive, public URL, or first-party artifact.

03
◇

Classification

Mark it verified, partial, pending, private, internal, or rejected. Uncertainty stays visible.

04
✓

Decision

Publish, correct, hold, or protect. The state is part of the evidence, not a footnote.
