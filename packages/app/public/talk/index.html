choose the correct route

One message. One lane. One next step.

Media

Interview, podcast, article, briefing

Send topic, format, audience, deadline, and whether claims need source review before publication.

Partnership

Pilot, institution, serious collaboration

Send the problem, proposed partner role, timeline, resource need, and first decision required.

StartOn

Youth-tech, hubs, mentors, CAPEX

Send municipality or partner context, target youth group, space need, budget blocker, and available materials.

Speaking

Event, panel, webinar, closed room

Send date, location, language, audience, format, visual requirements, and approval deadline.

Civic AI

Public systems, accountability, radar

Send the issue, public-interest angle, sources, stage, and what should be separated as fact versus analysis.

Evidence Review

Claim, metric, proof, public wording

Send the exact wording you want to publish and the source material that supports or limits it.

Use this structure

Copy the pattern.

Context: one paragraph. No biography dump.

Route: media, partnership, StartOn, speaking, civic AI, evidence review, or other.

Ask: the decision or output wanted.

Proof: links, screenshots, files, dates, or public records.

Next step: meeting, reply, review, quote, pilot, or publish gate.

כלל פעולה

פנייה טובה לא נשמעת מרגשת. היא מקדמת החלטה.

אם יש מקור — מצרפים. אם אין מקור — מסמנים לבדיקה. אם זה רגיש — לא שולחים כטקסט ציבורי. המטרה היא להפוך כל שיחה לנתיב עבודה, לא לערימה של רעש.

Rule: a strong message is not long. It is clear, useful, verifiable, and tied to one next move.
