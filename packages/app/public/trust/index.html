Founder note

Why this page exists.

7YA was built to separate public truth from noise — and to turn verified truth into public strength.

If a claim cannot be verified, it does not become a public fact. That does not weaken the story. It protects it, sharpens it, and makes it credible enough for media, partners, institutions, and serious public review.

evidence status labels

What can be claimed publicly?

Verified

Confirmed public fact

Supported by an approved source and ready for public use.

Source Visible

Source can be seen

A source exists or is visible, but the claim may still need capture, context, or review.

Source Pending

Not final

The claim is being checked and should not be presented as settled public fact.

Internal Only

Operational context

Useful for private planning, but not intended for publication.

Not for Publication

Do not publish

Private, sensitive, unverifiable, or inappropriate for public release.

Protected Mode

No raw technical failure

If AI, API, or hosting fails, users are routed to static intake and trust context.

Public Claims Policy

If a claim is not verified, it is not presented as a final public fact.

This is the core rule that keeps 7YA strong, not small.

What we do not publish

Private or weak material stays out.

Private contacts, family details, sensitive internal identifiers, unverifiable claims, rumors, and personal material do not enter the public surface.

Emergency UX rule: never expose raw HTTP 402 to visitors. Use protected mode, explain the fallback, and route the user to Talk, Evidence, or Contact.
