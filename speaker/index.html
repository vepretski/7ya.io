SHORT BIO

ביו קצר למפיקים.

Igor Vepretski is an Israeli founder, public-service veteran, creator, founder of StartOn and builder of 7YA. His work connects field experience in security and public systems with civic technology, youth impact, digital trust, public narrative and responsible AI. He speaks in Hebrew, English and Russian.
