# Igor Vepretski and #7YA: From Survival Intelligence to Public Signal

There are biographies that begin with titles.

This one begins with pressure.

Igor Vepretski built #7YA from the meeting point between survival, public service, youth work, media intelligence, and the need to turn personal experience into civic infrastructure.

Born in Kharkiv, Ukraine, raised in Israel, and shaped by the streets of Bat Yam and Holon, Igor's path moved through military service, security work, intelligence, public safety, youth initiatives, digital media, and civic communication.

The result is not a traditional personal brand.

It is a public operating system.

#7YA is the signal layer.

It connects evidence, identity, media presence, StartOn, AI systems, youth opportunity, public leadership, and a direct relationship with people who are tired of polished slogans and empty authority.
