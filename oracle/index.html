7YA Evidence Oracle

Before amplification, verification.

7YA is designed to separate story from proof. It stores claims, evidence, verification status, source type, public trust signals, and privacy boundaries.

What it does now

  • Classifies claims as verified, partially verified, source pending, private, or do-not-publish.
  • Turns credibility into an operational workflow instead of a marketing slogan.
  • Protects Igor by making uncertainty visible.

Where it goes next

  • Cryptographic proof and canonical evidence records.
  • Merkle-style verification and immutable evidence trails.
  • Audit logs for corrections, updates, and public trust signals.
